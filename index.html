Proposed ICRA 2027 workshop

Assurance Under Change

Keeping Learning-Enabled Humanoid Robots Safe After Deployment

Proposed date
24 or 28 May 2027 (one day; date TBC)
Location
Coex, Seoul, South Korea
Format
Full-day workshop

Final workshop acceptance and scheduling are pending.

About

Model updates, online adaptation, camera recalibration, and actuator wear can change a humanoid robot’s behavior after deployment. New objects, loads, tools, workflows, and operating environments can also change the assumptions supporting its safety claims.

This proposed workshop examines how to assess these changes and determine which earlier evidence remains applicable. Discussions will connect analysis, runtime checks, policy evaluation, and physical testing to decisions about continued operation, operating restrictions, rollback, and human involvement when the available evidence is insufficient.

Discussion questions

  1. Which safety assumptions, robot capabilities, and safety claims are affected by a change?
  2. Which earlier evidence can be reused, and when are new analyses, runtime checks, policy evaluations, or physical tests needed?
  3. When evidence is insufficient, should the robot continue operating, operate under restrictions, revert to an earlier configuration, or involve a human?

Planned format

The proposed full-day format includes six invited talks, contributed lightning talks, posters and demos, a small-group Assurance Decision Lab, and a closing panel. The detailed program is to be confirmed.
